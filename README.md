# Kayode Adeniyi

PhD Engineering researcher, [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/) (Shawki Saad Scholar),
on the VIEW project, funded by [Schmidt Sciences](https://www.schmidtsciences.org/).

**Focus: Uncertainty quantification for Edge AI. Put simply; how far can a measurement be trusted, and what is it worth to a decision?**

A growing share of environmental sensing now happens on small devices, where sensors and TinyML models
make predictions on the spot. My research measures how far each of those predictions can be trusted.
The people who rely on them shape that work: their experience decides how uncertainty is measured
and which mistakes matter most.

**My approach to science: Technology & Society.** This way of working is inspired by my kind supervisor
[Wouter Buytaert](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/people/w.buytaert)'s expertise in socio-hydrology,
which studies water and people as one system, by
the ever-inspiring [James Manyika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Manyika), whose career path led to his leadership
of Research, Labs, Technology & Society at Google, and by
[Demis Hassabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demis_Hassabis), the whiz kid doing the unusual!
